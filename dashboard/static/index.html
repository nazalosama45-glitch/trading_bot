What do these words mean?
Fake money / practice
The bot trades on a test account with play money. It can't touch your real Binance account.
Buying and selling
The bot buys a coin hoping the price will go up, then sells it later. If it sold higher than it bought, that's a profit.
Sells to limit loss
A safety exit. If the price falls to this number, the bot sells right away so a bad trade can't get worse.
Sells to keep profit
If the price rises to this number, the bot sells and keeps the gain.
Win rate
The share of trades that made money. 40 out of 100 is a 40% win rate. A low win rate can still be fine if the wins are bigger than the losses.
Total result
Every finished trade's gain or loss added together, in percent. Plus (green) means it's ahead, minus (red) means it's behind.
Learning
After every 20 finished trades, the bot studies what worked and what didn't, and adjusts how it picks trades.
